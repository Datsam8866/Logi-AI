拆解 / 熱設計分析

ComputeApple Mac Pro (Late 2013)

Thermal Design

Triangular unified thermal core: CPU mounts to one face, each GPU to other two faces via thermal paste. Air drawn from bottom through core, exhausted out top. Designed to eliminate multiple fans but locked GPU upgrade path.

Key ICs

Fan motor controller: Allegro Microsystems A5940LPT (3-phase sensorless sinusoidal driver)

PCB Overview

CPU: Intel Xeon E5-1620 v2 (Quad-Core 3.7GHz, 10MB L3); upgradable socket; Dual AMD FirePro GPU modules

Build Quality

Torx screws throughout; modular design; CPU user-replaceable; RAM easily accessible; fan replaceable. Repairability score: 8/10 (iFixit)

Note: Category Compute is user-defined, not in default VC taxonomy. Known thermal corner issue: GPU failures due to heat buildup in sealed cylinder design.

IR CameraFLIR One Pro
