## Notes & Gaps

Neat Bar Pro vs Neat Bar Gen2: Pro is ~4× longer (890 vs 198 mm), heavier (3.1 vs 0.52 kg), and targets larger rooms (>10 pax). Both run Neat OS. 3rd HDMI out requires Zoom Rooms — not available on MTR.
