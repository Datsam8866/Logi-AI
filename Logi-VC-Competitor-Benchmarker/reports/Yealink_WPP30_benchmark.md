## Notes & Gaps

Wireless content sharing dongle. Plugs into presenter laptop (USB-A or USB-C); transmits 4K@30fps to Yealink room display. Self-built WiFi 6 AP -- no external network router required. Supports up to 4 simultaneous content streams. 60g ultra-lightweight.
